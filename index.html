Research

Variational problems under variable-coefficient differential constraints

Current project, supervised by Irene Fonseca. Manuscript in preparation.

For integral energies whose admissible fields satisfy a linear differential constraint, weak lower semicontinuity is governed not by convexity but by 𝒜-quasiconvexity. P. M. Santos characterized it for variable coefficients under smooth coefficients and uniform constant rank, using pseudodifferential operators. I study both directions by elementary means under weaker hypotheses: sufficiency through a blow-up that freezes the coefficients, with Lipschitz coefficients and pointwise constant rank; and necessity by adapting the moving-kernel construction of Davoli and Fonseca, where the rank is locally constant along each lattice direction.

Stochastic tug-of-war games and the regularized p-Laplacian

M.S. thesis, advised by Juan J. Manfredi.

The value functions of a tug-of-war game with noise converge, as the step size vanishes, to solutions of a p-Laplace-type equation. The thesis rests on an affine lift that turns the regularized p-Laplace equation into the ordinary one in one more dimension, and develops it in two directions: a projected game whose values converge to the viscosity solution, and a pointwise asymptotic mean-value characterization of classical solutions. Both appear among the papers below.

Papers

Mathematics

  1. 2026

    A projected tug-of-war game for the regularized p-Laplacian

    Published in Journal of Elliptic and Parabolic Equations

    A stochastic-game interpretation of the regularized p-Laplace equation for p ≥ 2, built on an affine lift that turns it into the ordinary p-Laplace equation one dimension higher.

    More

    Projecting the higher-dimensional game back to the original domain gives a dynamic programming principle with strip boundary data. I prove existence, uniqueness and Borel measurability for it, identify its solution with the value of the projected game, and prove convergence to the viscosity solution as the step size tends to zero. The lift introduces artificial caps on the cylinder; the compactness estimates are arranged to stay away from them.

    Journal version arXiv PDF

  2. 2026

    An asymptotic mean value characterization for the regularized p-Laplacian

    Preprint

    For C2 functions, solving the regularized p-Laplace equation is equivalent to a pointwise asymptotic mean-value identity, throughout 1 < p < ∞.

    More

    The identity combines tilted extrema with a weighted local average. Because the lifted gradient never vanishes, no separate treatment of critical points is needed, unlike the classical p-harmonic theory. An example built from Aronsson's function shows that the identity fails pointwise at the formal endpoint p = ∞. Convergence of the associated game for p ≥ 2 is the subject of the companion paper.

    arXiv PDF

  3. 2025

    Extended set difference: an inverse operation of Minkowski summation

    With Arie Beresteanu

    Accepted in Journal of Convex Analysis

    Minkowski addition has no exact inverse in general. We define a difference of compact convex sets by minimizing the Hausdorff distance between A and B + X.

    More

    We prove that a minimizer exists for every pair of nonempty compact convex sets. Minimizers need not be unique, so we study the whole collection: bounds on its size, stability under transformations and perturbations, and a linear-optimization method for computing it.

    arXiv PDF

  4. 2025

    Optimal control of an epidemic with intervention design

    Preprint

    Optimal vaccination in an SEIR model with a hard healthcare-capacity constraint, an infinite horizon, and a delay between decision and implementation.

    More

    Moreau–Yosida regularization approximates the hard constraint by penalized problems, and Γ-convergence relates finite- and infinite-horizon solutions. Pontryagin conditions characterize the intervention strategies, including boundary arcs on which infections stay at the capacity limit, and numerical examples measure the cost of delay.

    arXiv PDF

Econometrics and statistics

  1. 2026

    Partial identification with auxiliary moment restrictions

    With Arie Beresteanu

    Preprint

    How known population aggregates restrict the sharp identification region of the best linear predictor when outcomes are observed only as intervals.

    More

    The added information constrains the admissible completions of the data without changing the reported intervals. Some restrictions reduce the dimension of the region; others narrow it without reducing its dimension. The paper derives directional measures of identifying value and applies them to interval-valued wages in the Current Population Survey.

    arXiv PDF

  2. 2026

    Public good provision under locally private signals

    With Jordan Awan

    Preprint

    Optimal public-good provision when preferences are observed only through a privacy channel that randomizes individual reports.

    More

    The optimal allocation is determined by an aggregate posterior score combining expected valuations and virtual values. The implementing transfers solve a Fredholm integral equation: completeness gives uniqueness when a solution exists, while existence needs a separate range condition. Comparisons between privacy channels depend on how their privacy levels are calibrated.

    arXiv PDF

  3. 2025

    Bounds for restricted selections of random sets

    With Arie Beresteanu

    Preprint

    Sharp bounds on the means, medians and event probabilities attainable by selections of a random interval that satisfy aggregate restrictions.

    More

    On a non-atomic probability space we characterize the restricted selection sets and derive the bounds by rearrangement and convex duality. For random compact convex sets in higher dimensions, an approximation by unions of random cubes reduces the problems to coordinate-wise interval problems.
